# EXP-3-PROMPT-ENGINEERING-

## Aim
To examine and compare the performance of different AI platforms, including ChatGPT, Claude, Google Gemini, Cohere Command, and Meta AI, by using a common text summarization task. The experiment evaluates the platforms based on accuracy, clarity, completeness, readability, and response time.

## Objectives
- To understand how different AI platforms respond to an identical prompt.
- To study the importance of effective prompt engineering.
- To evaluate the quality and usefulness of AI-generated summaries.
- To identify the advantages and limitations of different AI tools.

## Theory
Prompt Engineering is the process of creating clear and specific instructions to guide AI models in generating better responses. A properly designed prompt helps the AI understand the task and produce relevant, accurate, and well-organized results.

For example:

**Weak Prompt:** "Explain Artificial Intelligence."

**Effective Prompt:** "Explain Artificial Intelligence in 5 simple points with suitable examples for a beginner."

AI platforms use different models and training methods. Therefore, their responses may vary in terms of language, accuracy, structure, and completeness, even when the same prompt is provided.

## Tools Required
- ChatGPT
- Claude
- Google Gemini
- Cohere Command
- Meta AI
- Internet connection
- Web browser or mobile application

## Algorithm
1. Start the experiment.
2. Select five different AI platforms.
3. Choose text summarization as the common task.
4. Prepare a paragraph containing important information about Artificial Intelligence.
5. Enter the same prompt into all five AI platforms.
6. Collect and save the generated responses.
7. Evaluate the responses based on accuracy, clarity, completeness, readability, and speed.
8. Compare the results of all platforms.
9. Identify the strengths and weaknesses of each platform.
10. Record the final observations and conclusion.
11. Stop the experiment.

## Input Text
Artificial Intelligence (AI) is becoming an important part of modern technology. It is widely used in areas such as healthcare, education, banking, and transportation. AI helps machines process information, identify patterns, perform repetitive activities, and assist humans in making decisions. Generative AI has further expanded its capabilities by producing text, images, music, and computer programs. Many organizations use AI to increase efficiency and improve their services. However, AI also creates certain risks, including data privacy issues, unfair algorithmic decisions, unemployment, and ethical concerns. Proper monitoring, transparency, and responsible usage are necessary to ensure that AI technology is beneficial to society.

## Prompt
"Read the given paragraph and summarize it in exactly 50 words. Include the central idea and important supporting information. Use simple and clear English that can be easily understood by a first-year engineering student."

Artificial Intelligence is helping different industries work more efficiently. It can analyze data, automate tasks, and generate content like text and images. AI makes work easier but may cause problems involving privacy, fairness, and employment. Responsible use and proper monitoring are important for its safe development.

### 4. Cohere Command
AI supports various industries by analyzing data, automating tasks, and assisting decisions. Generative AI produces digital content such as text and images. It improves efficiency but creates concerns about privacy, fairness, employment, and ethics. Responsible development and monitoring are essential.

### 5. Meta AI

However, it also introduces challenges involving privacy, employment, fairness, and ethical responsibility. Proper monitoring and transparency help ensure its safe and effective use.

## Result
The experiment was successfully performed by comparing five AI platforms using a common text summarization prompt. Each platform generated responses with different levels of clarity, completeness, and readability. ChatGPT and Claude provided well-organized summaries, Gemini offered beginner-friendly explanations, Cohere Command focused on concise responses, and Meta AI delivered a balanced summary. Thus, the experiment demonstrates the importance of prompt engineering in improving AI-generated responses and understanding the differences between various AI platforms.
